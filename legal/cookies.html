Cookie Policy

Draft — subject to legal review before publication Last updated: September 2026

What we use

This website uses only essential storage: a secure session token that keeps members signed in, and a language preference stored in your browser. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Essential storage

Session storage is created only when you sign in and is removed when you sign out or after a period of inactivity. Your language choice (English or Arabic) is remembered on your device only.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash; these providers may receive your IP address when the assets load. Our authentication provider processes sign-in codes on our behalf.

Managing storage

You can clear site data at any time from your browser settings. Clearing it will sign you out and reset your language preference.
