Learn before you invest — anywhere

Plain-language guidance for anyone considering any investment in the UAE. Education only — not advice, and not an offer.

  • Verify the licence first

    Any party offering investments to the public in the UAE must be licensed by the Securities & Commodities Authority (SCA) or the Central Bank. Always verify a licence before committing any money — to anyone.

  • Understand how returns really work

    Real business profits vary month to month — trade margins move with demand, shipping and prices. No genuine business earns a smooth fixed percentage every single month.

  • Question "guaranteed returns"

    Promises of fixed, high monthly returns are the most common sign of investment fraud worldwide. If it sounds too good to be true, treat it as a warning — not an opportunity.

  • Read every agreement

    Understand the terms, the risks, and your exit rights before signing anything. If you cannot get clear written answers, walk away and seek independent professional advice.
